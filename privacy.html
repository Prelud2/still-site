Privacy policy

Effective 22 September 2026

Still is made by Santos & Halp Inc. The short version: Still does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

What stays on your device

Your saved mixes, the current mix, volume levels, timer choices and settings are stored on your device only. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What leaves your device

Nothing is sent to us. Still has no server, no accounts, no sign-in and no network features. All sound is generated locally.

Purchases

Still is free to use. The optional Still Plus subscription or lifetime purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple Account, card, or billing details, and no purchase information is sent to us. Apple's handling of purchase data is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

The app contains no ads, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and no tracking. It does not use the advertising identifier.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will change. Questions: santosfjseo@gmail.com.
